# Shikoku, car-free and cheap — two options

Built only from sourced material in `research/`. Every time and fare here is either a
published operator figure or a named traveller's account; anything I could not source
says **not stated**. **No travel time in this document is estimated by me.**

---

## The shape of the problem

Both options run the **same spine** once they're on Shikoku, because the island's
timetables leave very little choice:

```
                    ┌─ OPTION A: Himeji → Shodoshima → Takamatsu ─┐
   OSAKA ───────────┤                                             ├── TOKUSHIMA
                    └─ OPTION B: Awaji → Naruto ─────────────────┘      │
                                                                        │
   TOKUSHIMA ──(Tokushima Line, all-local, 1h55, ¥1,830, 0 changes)──► AWA-IKEDA
        │                                                                │
        └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────► IYA VALLEY
                                                                         │
   IYA ──(Dosan local 08:15 or 14:22)──► KOCHI ──(bus ¥4,000)──► MATSUYAMA
                                                                         │
   MATSUYAMA ──► IMABARI ──► SHIMANAMI ──► ONOMICHI ──► MIHARA ──► TADANOUMI
                                                          (🐰 Okunoshima)
                                                                         │
                                          ──► HIROSHIMA ──► MIYAJIMA ──► KYUSHU
```

**Why the spine is fixed:**

| Rule | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| **Enter Iya from Tokushima, not Kōchi** | Awa-Ikeda → Tokushima all-local is **1h55, ¥1,830, zero changes** (11 locals/day vs 3 tokkyu). The reverse — Ōboke ↔ Kōchi — has **only 4 locals a day.** |
| **Leave Iya southward on the 08:15 or 14:22** | Those are two of only four southbound locals from Ōboke. Miss them and the next is 18:19. |
| **Kōchi → Matsuyama must be the bus** | All-local rail is **9h58, ¥5,380, one daily thread departing 05:39.** The bus is **~3h, ¥4,000 — or ¥3,000 on showing a Seishun 18 kippu.** |
| **Exit westward, not back to Okayama** | Matsuyama → Imabari → Onomichi → Mihara → Tadanoumi is linear. Going via Takamatsu/Okayama doubles back. |
| **Counter-clockwise, always** | Every trip report converges on Takamatsu-first / Matsuyama-last, because Matsuyama has the onward ferry and Shimanami. |

## ⭐ OPTION A — "Himeji first" (13 nights)

Her stated shape: Himeji → Shodoshima 1–2 nights → Tokushima → onward.
**This is the stronger option.** Shodoshima sits naturally on the way in, the ferry
chain is clean, and nothing backtracks.

### Days 1–2 · Osaka → Himeji → Shodoshima

**Day 1 — Osaka → Himeji → Fukuda → Tonoshō**

| Leg | Detail | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Osaka → Himeji | **JR Shin-Kaisoku, 1h03–06, no surcharge, every 15 min** | **¥1,460** |
| Bags | **JR Himeji North Exit lockers: small ¥400 / med ¥500 / large ¥700, IC card, 24 h** | ¥400–700 |
| Castle + Koko-en | **20 min walk** from the North Exit (you can see the castle as you exit) | **¥2,600 combo** |
| Himeji Stn → Port | **Shinki Bus route 94, platform 1, North Exit. ~20–30 min, ~2/hour, ¥320** | ¥320 |
| Himeji → Fukuda | **Shodoshima Ferry, 100 min.** Dep. 07:15 / 09:45 / 11:15 / **13:35** / 15:10 / 17:25 / 19:30 | **¥2,100** |
| Fukuda → Tonoshō | **Olive Bus South Fukuda Line, ~1 h** ⚠️ **the connection at Fukuda is a 5-minute transfer** | ¥500 |

**Sleep: Tonoshō.** Ferry hub, bus network origin, walkable to Angel Road, has a
share-cycle station. **Yoko Inn** — u/lemonbiscuits (Nov 2025): *"friendly owner,
comfortable room, affordable price… 10pm curfew and bathroom was shared."*
Or **Okido Hotel**, 「土庄港より徒歩1分」.

> **Timing:** the **13:35 ferry** is the sweet spot — it leaves a full morning for the
> castle and lands you with daylight. The **09:45** is the one real travellers used
> (it connects to the 11:45 Fukuda→Tonoshō bus) if you'd rather do Himeji as a
> separate day trip from Osaka beforehand.

**Day 2 — Shodoshima, day pass**

Buy the **Olive Bus 1-day pass, ¥1,600** (break-even is two ¥500 rides; you'll do more).
⚠️ **¥2,000 during the Setouchi Triennale**, when a journalist who toured the island
car-free described the 16:28 return as 「すし詰め状態」 — packed like sushi.

- **Kankakei Gorge** — Sakate Line to **Kusakabe Port (~30 min)**, then the
  **free town shuttle, 11 min, 8 runs/day** (⚠️ this replaced the old bus in **March
  2025**; japan-guide still describes the old arrangement). Ropeway **¥2,340 return**
  (¥2,700 in November), every 12 min. **Worked real connection:** Kusakabe 10:11 →
  shuttle 10:30 → Kouuntei 10:41 → ropeway 10:47; back down 12:12 → 12:30 → 12:41.
- **Olive Park** — alight **「サン・オリーブ」, zero walk.** Flat, open 08:30–17:00
  daily, reachable in one bus from every ferry port. Has a share-cycle station.
- **Marukin Soy Sauce Museum** — **Sakate Line, alight 「丸金前」, immediately outside.**
  ¥500. Or **Yamaroku**, free tour, no reservation. ⚠️ 醤の郷 shuts 16:00–17:00.

**Day 3 — Angel Road, then Shikoku**

- **Angel Road: just walk, ~30 min from Tonoshō Port** (official). The bus is only 5
  runs/day, so walking is often faster. ⚠️ **Tide-dependent** — two ~6-hour windows a
  day; official table at <https://www.shikokuferry.com/angel>.
- **Tonoshō → Takamatsu: the car ferry, ¥700, 60 min, 15 sailings/day** (first 06:36,
  last 20:40). **Not** the high-speed boat at ¥1,400 for 25 minutes saved.

⚠️ **Do NOT book the Jumbo Ferry from Sakate** unless you mean to — it docks at
**Takamatsu-HIGASHI Port, ~15 min from the walkable Takamatsu Port.** This is the most
common car-free mistake in the area.

**Sleep: Takamatsu.** **WeBase 高松**, mixed dorm **from ¥3,500** — and there's a
**backpacker weekly plan at ¥2,600/night (¥18,200 for 7)** if they end up lingering.
It's in Kawaramachi, one Kotoden stop from the port. A repeat guest calls it
「高松での鉄板ドミトリー」. Alternative for ferry access: **紺屋町ゲストハウス久々**,
「ＪＲ高松駅や高松港まで徒歩１０分圏内」.

### Day 4 · Takamatsu

**Ritsurin Garden** — universally the most-praised single sight in these trip reports
(marc2002: *"best part imo"*; u/turtledude100: *"the prettiest gardens in Japan"*).
10-minute train from the station. Flat.
⚠️ Takamatsu is *"virtually dead"* by 21:30 — eat early.

### Day 5 · Takamatsu → Tokushima

**Takamatsu → Tokushima: 高徳エクスプレス bus, ¥2,300, ~1h50, 12 departures each way**
— the cheapest and fastest link, and a 4-ride book is ¥8,000.

**Sleep: Tokushima. HOSTEL PAQ**, **8-minute walk from JR Tokushima Station**, 8.4–8.5
from ~2,700 ratings, bicycle rental, big with 88-temple pilgrims. A guest:
*"Location was perfect to the train station… various eateries and convenience stores."*

**Optional add-on here:** the **first five temples of the 88-temple pilgrimage** are an
easy flat walk from local stations — u/Yourbass walked them: *"There were signs all
along the way."* u/a7sharp9 confirms they're *"easily [done] with local train
connections."* A good low-effort half-day.

### Days 6–7 · Iya Valley

**Day 6 — Tokushima → Awa-Ikeda → Ōboke**

- **Tokushima → Awa-Ikeda: all-local, 1h55, ¥1,830, ZERO changes.** Departures 05:43,
  06:25, 06:52, 07:52, 09:34, 12:37, 15:40, 16:56, 18:17, 19:51, 21:19.
- **Awa-Ikeda → Ōboke: local, ~30 min, ¥530.** ⚠️ **Only 7 arrivals a day** — 06:02,
  08:14, 12:50, 14:18, 17:23, 18:18, 19:42. There is a **4h30 midday hole**; build the
  day around the 08:14 or the 12:50.
- **Drop bags at Awa-Ikeda, not Ōboke.** Awa-Ikeda has a **¥400 size and a 113 cm tall
  unit, 5:30–23:30, and is staffed**; Ōboke is **completely unstaffed since 2010** and
  every locker is ¥500, coins only, no change machine. **Bring ¥100 coins.**
  *(Cheapest of all: **Kazurabashi Yumebutai, ¥100**, 09:00–16:00.)*

**Afternoon — Ōboke Gorge boat.** ⭐ The single best no-effort sight in the valley:
**¥1,500, 30 minutes, 9:00–17:00 (last boat 16:30), open year-round, no reservation.**
**Don't walk the 25 minutes** — the bus stops at the door (**Ōboke Stn → 大歩危峡,
4 min, ¥110**), and the operator shuttles train arrivals for free
(「電車でお越しのお客様は大歩危駅まで送迎いたします」, 0883-84-1211).
⚠️ A father who walked it warns there is **no riverside path — you use the narrow main
road with heavy traffic.**

**Sleep: Awa-Ikeda.** Eli Fessler used **4S STAY**, run by JR Shikoku. This is the
smart car-free base — sleeping inside Iya means either an expensive ryokan (~¥20,000pp
with dinner) or camping (**Kazurabashi Camp Village ¥750**).

**Day 7 — the vine bridge day**

**Ōboke Stn → Kazurabashi Yumebutai, 21–28 min, ¥670, 8 departures/day:**
08:58 · 10:07 · 11:08 · 11:58 · 13:10 · 14:53 · 16:08 · 17:08
**Back, 8/day:** 08:11 · 09:11 · 11:13 · 12:11 · 13:11 · 14:16 · 15:11 · **17:41 (last)**

The no-hiking shortlist, in one loop:
- **Iya Kazurabashi vine bridge** — 45 m long, 14 m above the river, **¥550**,
  **5-minute walk from the bus stop**. One-way crossing only. ⚠️ 夢舞台 and かずら橋
  are **different stops, 10–15 min apart on foot** — use かずら橋.
- **Biwa Falls** — 50 m high, **"just about 50 metres to the left after crossing the
  bridge."** Two minutes.
- **Hotel Iya Onsen cable car** — 170 m drop, 42° incline, **5 minutes**, best
  no-effort view in the valley. Day bathing ¥1,700 (indoor only ¥700).
  ⚠️ **Only 3 buses/day** serve this stop (arrive 10:49 / 13:52 / 16:34) and the
  turnarounds are 3 minutes — so either commit ~3 hours or skip it.

**❌ Skip Ochiai village** despite the photos: it climbs **400 vertical metres**, and
the famous viewpoint is **on the opposite side of the valley, effectively taxi-only.**

**⚠️ The season fork.** Everything east of Kubo — **Nagoro scarecrow village, the
Oku-Iya double bridges, Mt Tsurugi** — runs **weekends and holidays only in season and
is completely closed 24 Nov – 31 Mar.** If they're going in winter, that whole wing is
off the table and one Iya day is enough. If they're going in season on a weekend, add a
day: Kubo transfer, then the city bus (Kubo 10:20 → Tsurugi 11:10; back 14:55).
⚠️ The Mt Tsurugi chairlift is **¥2,300 return** and closed 1 Dec – 29 Apr.

⚠️ **Iya buses are cash only, coins and ¥1,000 notes.** Break big notes at the
Awa-Ikeda Seven-Eleven first. **All services suspended 1 January.**
⚠️ There is **no English timetable** on the operator's site — print the Miyoshi City
one: <https://miyoshi-tourism.jp/download/?file=bus_timetable_en>

### Days 8–9 · Kōchi

**Ōboke → Kōchi on a local: 08:15 or 14:22.** (The other two southbound locals,
06:02 and 18:19, terminate short at Tosa-Yamada.) **~¥1,430 IC.**

Kōchi is flat, walkable and cheap: **Hirome Market**, the **Sunday morning market**,
the castle, **Tosaden trams (¥500 city-zone day pass)**, and the **MY-YU bus pass**
(¥1,000, halved to ¥600 on showing a foreign passport) for Katsurahama and Makino.

⚠️ **Kōchi rejects national IC cards** — it has its own (Iruca). **Carry cash.**
⚠️ **Cruise-ship mornings:** u/lindoreda found the MY-YU bus *"standing in a sardine
can"* from Harimayabashi — **board at the JR station terminus instead**, and go in the
afternoon.
⚠️ **Makino Botanical Garden** *"is on the side of a mountain so it's not quite the
easy stroll you expect."* Kōchi Castle has ladder-stairs inside.
⚠️ **Hirome Market** splits opinion: *"very crowded n loud"*, *"I got overstimulated"*,
*"Surprisingly bad."* Go early or eat at the stalls outside.

**Sleep: Kōchi city centre**, near Hirome — Dormy Inn (onsen, laundry, free night
ramen), JR Clement Inn, Comfort Hotel or Kochi Pacific all sit central.

### Days 10–11 · Matsuyama

**Kōchi → Matsuyama: なんごくエクスプレス bus, ~3 h, ¥4,000 — or ¥3,000 on showing a
Seishun 18 kippu** (documented on JR Shikoku Bus's own page). 5 round trips: Kōchi dep.
8:30 / 10:20 / 13:20 / 16:30 / 18:20. Web coupon books drop it to ¥2,000/ride at 10.

**Dogo Onsen** — ✅ **the renovations are finished** as of July 2025, after years under
what one traveller's husband called the "modesty tarp."
⚠️ The foot bath overlooking it is *"blisteringly hot… extremely hot, always."*

**Matsuyama Castle** — ⭐ **take the chair lift, not the ropeway: the wait is much
shorter.** ⚠️ The keep is an original: *"no elevators, very steep and narrow stairs,
and you're doing this in socks."* The grounds and the view are the point; the interior
is optional. **Stock up on water before going up** — drinks cost 20–30 yen more at the
top and the orange-juice taps are double.

✅ **Matsuyama now accepts Suica** (since ~July 2025) — a real improvement on the rest
of Shikoku. Tram is a flat fare with a small IC discount.

**Sleep: near JR Matsuyama Station or Dogo.** **Guesthouse Casablanca**, near Matsuyama
Station, was **¥2,500** in 2020. **REF by VESSEL** was used by u/lemonbiscuits (who also
used it for luggage storage). ⚠️ **Matsuyama Youth Hostel** got the trip's only bad
review: *"the room was not very clean."*

**Optional day trips** (both praised, both flat): **Uchiko and Ōzu** by train —
u/turtledude100: *"extremely quiet and extremely beautiful places."* And **Shimonada
Station** for the sea-level platform photo — ⚠️ but it's **one train an hour each way**;
marc2002 *"got to wait abt 2hrs for the train back"* and froze. Make it a round trip
from Matsuyama, or skip it.

### Days 12–13 · The exit — Shimanami, then the rabbits

**Day 12 — Matsuyama → Imabari → Onomichi**

- **Matsuyama → Imabari: local ~71 min, ¥1,080** (limited express is 37 min for
  ¥1,840 — the local is the obvious call here).
- **Imabari → Onomichi: ¥2,390, 1.5–2 hours, hourly connections** — Shimanami Liner to
  **Innoshima Ōhashi**, then a local bus (~18/day). ⚠️ The operator's own partner site
  warns *"Transferring buses at Innoshima Ohashi BS may not be smooth"* — long waits,
  nothing at the stop.
- **⚠️ There is NO direct Imabari→Onomichi Shimanami Liner** — that route was abolished
  in **2005**, and Setouchi Bus's own transfer-guide page currently 404s.

**The nicer version, if they fancy it:** rent a bike (**¥3,000/day + ¥1,000 one-way
drop-off**, bridge tolls **waived for cyclists through 31 March 2028**) and ride part
of it, then bus or ferry the rest. The precedent is exact: one blogger took a
**~1-hour bus Imabari → Ōmishima** *specifically because of bus service availability*,
rode three bridges, and bussed back. Another rode ~30 km and took the **Setoda →
Onomichi ferry (¥1,500 + ¥500 for the bike, 6 sailings/day)** back.
⚠️ **Every bridge means a sustained climbing ramp**, and the full 70 km takes beginners
*"eight to ten hours."* **Ride a section, don't commit to the whole thing.**

**Sleep: Onomichi. Guesthouse Yadocurly — ¥2,800/night**, 4-bed mixed dorm.

**Day 13 — Onomichi → Okunoshima 🐰 → Hiroshima**

- **Mihara → Tadanoumi: JR Kure Line local, 22 min, ¥320.**
  *(Onomichi → Mihara time and fare: **not stated** in any source I could reach.)*
- **Tadanoumi Station → port: 3–5 minutes' walk.**
- **Luggage: ¥500 per bag per day at the port**, open during ferry hours, 365 days —
  confirmed by a traveller who paid exactly that.
- **⚠️ BUY RABBIT FOOD BEFORE BOARDING.** It is *"no longer sold on the island."*
  ~**¥200** at the Rabbit Island store by the station, open 07:00–19:45.
  **Pellets, not vegetables** — the official rules say leftovers spoil and attract crows.
- **Ferry ~15 min, roughly every 30–45 min. CASH ONLY.** Two operators, which is why
  sources disagree: **Kyukamura passenger boat ¥310**, **Omishima car ferry ¥360**.
  Last departure from Tadanoumi for day visitors: **18:30**.
- **⚠️ Three hours is not enough.** Nick & Jerry allocated three, *"ended up hustling
  back to the port,"* and concluded they *"really needed like 5 hours."* Budget 4–5.
- On the island: Poison Gas Museum ¥150, onsen day-use ¥550, free shuttle from the pier
  to the hotel, or a 15-minute walk. **One restaurant only** — bring food.

**Optional 1-hour stop: Takehara old town**, on the same Kure Line, **12 minutes' walk
from the station.** Edo-period salt-and-sake merchant houses, and the Nikka Whisky
founder's museum. Nobody calls it a highlight; nobody regrets an hour there.

- **Tadanoumi → Hiroshima:** ⭐ **Geiyo Bus かぐや姫号, ¥1,500, 1 h 40 min, 4 round
  trips/day**, toilets and power outlets — beats the 2-hour Kure Line local (¥1,320) on
  time and comfort at nearly the same price.

**Sleep: Hiroshima.** Hiroden is a **flat ¥240** anywhere, so central beats
station-side. **The Evergreen Hostel** is called *"the best cheap hostel in Hiroshima,"*
Peace Park and Dome *"just 3-minutes away on foot."* **J-Hoppers** is 3 min from Dobashi
tram stop, 5 min to the Peace Park, with a 24-h kitchen and rooftop terrace.

### Then: Miyajima → Kyushu

**⭐ Buy the Hiroden 一日乗車乗船券, ¥1,000.** It covers **all trams + the Miyajima
Matsudai ferry + the ¥100 visitor tax + discounts the Misen ropeway from ¥2,000 to
¥1,500.** One pass does the whole day.

**⭐ Free upgrade:** ride the **JR ferry outbound between 9:10 and 16:10** for the
**大鳥居便 torii close-up route** — same ¥200 fare, same 10 minutes, no extra cost.
Either operator back. (JR runs 5:00–22:42; Matsudai only 07:15–20:35.)

**Mt Misen, honestly** — this matters given they don't like hiking. The ropeway is
**¥2,000 return** (¥1,500 with the Hiroden pass), two stages with a mandatory transfer,
~20 min. **⚠️ Last ascent 16:00.** But the ropeway's **own page** says
「弥山山頂へはさらに往復約1時間かかります」 — **about an hour round trip on top**, and
japan-guide calls it *"about a 30 minute walk up to the summit along a rather steep
hiking trail,"* ~1 km with 100 m of climbing, confirmed by four independent sources.

> **Recommendation: ride the ropeway, enjoy Shishiiwa observatory, treat the summit as
> optional.** Shishiiwa is itself an observatory with Inland Sea views.

**⚠️ Miyajima's Reikado hall burned down on 20 May 2026.** Ropeway and Misen access
reopened the next day, but **the famous 1,200-year "eternal flame" hall is gone**
pending rebuild. Set expectations now.

**Should they sleep on the island?** The case for, from someone who did: once day
visitors leave by 5 pm *"the atmosphere changed completely"*; early mornings
*"especially peaceful"* with the torii reflected in still water, and you see the gate at
**different tidal states**. The case against, from the Hiroshima forum: *"if it's just
a bed for the night then return to Hiroshima as you will have more choices."*
⚠️ If they stay: **evening dining on the island is scarce — book dinner with the
accommodation.** Budget options: **Miyajima Guest House Mikuniya**, **Sakuraya Ryokan**.

## OPTION B — "Awaji & the whirlpools first" (14 nights)

Her stated shape: Osaka → Awaji Island → Naruto whirlpools → Shikoku, **and still fit
Shodoshima in.**

**I'll be straight about this one: it costs more, it's more fragile, and Shodoshima
doesn't fit cleanly.** It buys two things Option A can't: the whirlpools, and an
island almost no foreign backpacker sees. Whether that's worth it is genuinely her
call — but she should make it knowing the trade.

### ⚠️ Read this before anything else: the tide problem

The whirlpools only appear around **peak tidal flow**, twice a day, and the window
**scales with the tide's strength**:

> 「観潮には、この時刻を中心に、**大潮は前後２時間、中潮は前後１時間半、小潮は前後１時間**
> までが最適です。」 — <https://www.uzunomichi.jp/tide-calendar/>

**Spring tide (大潮, red on the calendar) = ±2 hours. Neap tide = ±1 hour.**
The operator's own disclaimer: 「時間によっては渦潮の巻かない時間帯もあり、その時間帯は
毎日変化します。」

**Real travellers who got it wrong:** a blog literally titled *"Naruto Whirlpool -
Visiting at the wrong timing"* — *"we had missed the best timing, which is supposed to
be from 11am to 1pm… All we saw was the overlapping of different waters."* A
TripAdvisor review titled **"No whirlpool at non-peak hours."** And even at peak,
reviewers report *"a few small swirls and a larger area of turbulent water"* — not the
brochure.

**⚠️ The carless trap:** one reviewer notes that if peak tide lands at 09:30 with
sailings at 09:00 and 09:40, **there may be no bus that gets you there in time.**

> **So: pick a red 大潮 day off the official calendar FIRST, note the peak time, then
> check it against the bus timetable, and build the whole day backwards from that.**
> Earliest possible arrival at 鳴門公園 is **07:40 on weekdays.**

### Days 1–3 · Osaka → Awaji

**Day 1 — getting there.** Two routes, and the cheap one depends on the 18 kippu:

| Route | Cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **⭐ With a Seishun 18:** JR free to **Akashi**, walk ~10 min to the port, **Jenova Line ferry to Iwaya** | **¥700** | **13 minutes**, ~15–16 sailings/day, first 05:40 last 23:40. Nicest arrival, and **Iwaya Port Terminal 1F sells the ¥880 bus day pass**. |
| Without: JR to **Maiko**, walk down to **高速舞子** under the bridge, bus | **¥600** to 淡路IC | ~half-hourly across four operators |
| Direct from Osaka | **¥1,850** to 淡路IC, **¥3,000** to Sumoto BC | かけはし号, 121 km, 2h41 |

**⚠️ Neither the JR Pass nor the Seishun 18 works on any Awaji bus or the Jenova
ferry.** JR's own page: *"The PASS is not valid for travel on express bus routes
operated by JR Bus."* Everything on Awaji is cash out of pocket.

**Days 2–3 — Awaji, and the hard truth about getting around it.**

**Awaji has no railway.** Getting *to* the island is easy; getting *around* it is not.
The one blogger who genuinely tried to roam it car-free ended up **walking 50 minutes
between bus stops**:
> 「どちらも便数が少なく、なかなか都合良くバスは来ないので、おのころ島神社から…
> 「市」というバス停まで**約50分歩きました**。」

And the network is hub-and-spoke, which catches people out:
> 「淡路交通のバスは洲本BCから**放射状**に路線が伸びているので、路線が異なる目的地に行く
> 場合は**一旦洲本BCに戻る必要があります**。」

> **⭐ The rule: commit to ONE corridor.** Either the **north-west** (Iwaya base, free
> Pasona shuttle + ¥1,000 あわ神あわ姫 day pass) **or Sumoto-as-hub** (¥880 Awaji Kōtsū
> day pass, 縦貫線 spine). Not both, not the whole island.

**Passes:** **淡路島フリークーポン ¥880/day** on Awaji Kōtsū island routes.
⚠️ **Cut from ¥1,400 on 20 July 2024 — older blogs are wrong.**
⚠️ **Island buses do not take IC cards** (PayPay reportedly works). **Carry cash.**
⚠️ **Plan on <https://busmo.656.ch/>, not Google Maps** — it's the official Awaji
planner covering 13 operators plus ferries.

**The 縦貫線 spine (Fukura ↔ Sumoto ↔ Tsuna) is genuinely usable:** 14–16 departures a
day, **47–59 min Fukura↔Sumoto, ¥500 max.**

**Sleep:** ⚠️ thin pickings. **AWAJI TOURIST TROPHY HOUSE**, 「五色バスセンターより
徒歩100m」 — the terminus of the 都志線 from Sumoto BC, so **reachable on the ¥880
pass**. Dorms and privates; third-party prices 相部屋 ¥2,800 / 個室 ¥3,400〜.
*(One source says 「徒歩だと20～30分」, conflicting with the 100 m — verify.)*
Or **Guesthouse Shimauta**, Shizuki — ⚠️ **its official page currently says dorm plans
are NOT bookable**, and 「当ゲストハウスには英語対応スタッフはおりません」.
**There is no hostel in Fukura and none next to Sumoto Bus Center.**

### Day 4 · Awaji → whirlpools → Tokushima

**The only through bus across the Ōnaruto Bridge is Awaji Kōtsū's 淡路・徳島線 —
4 runs/day weekdays, 3 on weekends.** Everything hinges on it.

**Weekday, from 洲本BC:**

### ⚠️ The Shodoshima problem in Option B

**There is no clean slot.** Shodoshima's only ferries go to Takamatsu, Okayama and
Kobe — nothing to Tokushima or the south. Three ways to solve it, none free:

| Fix | Cost | Trade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **① Detour from Takamatsu after Tokushima** — Tokushima → Takamatsu (bus ¥2,300) → Tonoshō (¥700) → 2 nights → back to Takamatsu → south to Kotohira/Iya | **+~¥6,000 pp and 3 days** | The northern dogleg. Cleanest of the three. |
| **② Drop Kōchi.** Iya → Kotohira → Takamatsu → Shodoshima → Takamatsu → Matsuyama all-local (**4.5–5 h, ¥3,960, 2 changes**) | **~neutral** | Loses Hirome Market and the Sunday market — but Kōchi is bus-dependent, cruise-ship-crowded, and the Kōchi→Matsuyama link is the island's worst. **A defensible cut.** |
| **③ Exit via Shodoshima** — Takamatsu → Tonoshō → **Shin-Okayama ferry ¥1,200, 70 min, 8/day** → Okayama → Mihara → Tadanoumi | cheap | ❌ **Loses Matsuyama, Dogo Onsen and Shimanami.** I don't recommend it. |

**My read: if she picks Option B, take fix ②.** Kōchi is the most cuttable thing on the
spine, and swapping it for Shodoshima keeps the trip linear.

## Which one?

**Option A, unless she specifically wants the whirlpools.** It is cheaper, the ferry
chain is clean, Shodoshima falls exactly where she wanted it, and nothing backtracks.
Himeji Castle is the one budget shock — **¥2,600 each since the March 2026 price rise**
— and skipping the keep interior is a fair call if that stings.

**Option B is a real trip, not a worse one** — but it costs more, hinges on a
**4-bus-a-day** line and a tide table, and forces a choice between Kōchi and
Shodoshima. Take it if the whirlpools and an unvisited island matter more than the
extra ~¥6,000 and the fragility.

**A middle path worth mentioning:** the 阿波エクスプレス buses from Kobe and Osaka
**also stop at 鳴門公園口** — so she can do the whirlpools *without touching Awaji at
all*, on a bus that runs ~15 times a day instead of four. That gets Option A's clean
logistics with Option B's headline sight.

## Things to verify before booking

- **Which month are they going?** The Iya season fork (24 Nov – 31 Mar) and the
  Seishun 18 windows both turn on it.
- **Kazurabashi vine-bridge winter closure** — it shut **9 Jan – 15 Feb** in 2023-24 for
  vine replacement; current-year dates not published.
- **Fukuda Olive Bus departure times** — the operator's PDFs are unextractable.
  **Get the printed timetable at the port.**
- **The Himeji–Shodoshima–Takamatsu through ticket** — a traveller calls it *"a great
  deal"*; price not published. **Ask at the Himeji Port window.**
- **Onomichi → Mihara** local train time and fare — not published anywhere I reached.
