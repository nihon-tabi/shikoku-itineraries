## 1. Seishun 18 Kippu (青春18きっぷ) — current rules

The late-2024 rewrite (first change since 1984) **has settled — no further change for
2025–26.** Tabiris headlines its 2026 piece 「『青春18きっぷ』新ルールが定着」.

| Item | Current rule |
| --- | --- |
| Types | 3-day **¥10,000**, 5-day **¥12,050** (same price adult/child) |
| Consecutive days | **Yes — mandatory.** Nominate a start date at purchase. No skipping. |
| Sharing | **One person per ticket.** (Previously up to 5 could share.) |
| Gates | Now works at **automatic gates** — the one genuine improvement |
| Trains | JR **local (普通) and rapid (快速)** ordinary non-reserved, all JR companies. No shinkansen, no limited express. |
| Season-locked | A ticket bought in one season can't be used in another |

[tabiris 2026 guide](https://tabiris.com/archives/seishun18-2026/) ·
[japan-guide](https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e2362.html) ·
[鉄道チャンネル](https://tetsudo-ch.com/13008280.html)

### 2026 validity windows (exact dates, tabiris)

| Season | Usable | On sale (3-day) | On sale (5-day) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | **1 Mar – 10 Apr** | 13 Feb – 8 Apr | 13 Feb – 6 Apr |
| Summer | **18 Jul – 8 Sep** | 3 Jul – 6 Sep | 3 Jul – 4 Sep |
| Winter | **11 Dec – 11 Jan 2027** | 27 Nov – 9 Jan 2027 | 27 Nov – 7 Jan 2027 |

⚠️ japan-guide still gives generic "Jul 20 – Sep 10 / Dec 10 – Jan 10" windows —
**tabiris's dated 2026 figures are the specific ones and they differ.**

### Shikoku coverage
- **JR Shikoku local trains: YES**, all lines.
- **Private railways: NO** — Kotoden, Iyotetsu, Tosaden, Asa Kaigan Railway (incl.
  DMV) and **Tosa Kuroshio Railway** all excluded.
- ⚠️ **The Kubokawa trap:** to ride the Yodo Line you must traverse
  **Kubokawa–Wakai (2.8 km), which is Tosa Kuroshio Railway track, not JR.
  You pay ¥210 cash** to the driver (2025 rate). Carry small change.
  [Shikoku Rail Note](https://shikoku-railway-note.com/seishun18kipu-tosa-kuroshio-railway/)
- **Tosa Kuro Marugoto Kippu** — ¥2,000 for 3 consecutive days on both Tosa
  Kuroshio lines, sold on proof of a valid JR Shikoku pass **including Seishun 18**
  (stated valid through March 2026 — re-verify).
- **Mugi Line bus hack:** under the JR Shikoku ⇄ Tokushima Bus 共同経営,
  **JR tickets including the Seishun 18 are valid on the Tokushima Bus highway bus
  between Anan and Kaifu-Kōkō-mae**, subject to seat availability, **no advance
  reservation possible**.
  [Shikoku Rail Note](https://shikoku-railway-note.com/muki-line-tokushima-bus-kyodo-keiei/)
- 🔥 **Kōchi–Matsuyama bus discount:** JR Shikoku Bus's official なんごくエクスプレス
  page states **「★ 片道3,000円でご利用できる！【青春18きっぷ提示割引】」** — show a valid
  Seishun 18 and the bus is **¥3,000** instead of ¥4,000.
  [official](https://www.jr-shikokubus.co.jp/businfo/nangoku_ex/matsuyama.html)
  ⚠️ Japan Cheapo and a 2022 blog both claim ¥1,000 — **stale/wrong for 2026.**

### JR Miyajima Ferry
**Covered** (it's JR West). Fare would otherwise be ¥200 one-way. ⚠️ Since
1 Oct 2023 you must **separately buy the 宮島訪問税 ticket** — the 18 kippu covers
the fare but not the ¥100 tax. **Not** valid on the competing Matsudai ferry.

### What real users say about the consecutive-day rule
- **Hard number:** sales fell from **~620,000 (FY2023) to ~410,000 (FY2024)** — a
  ~34% drop — per Yomiuri Shimbun, cited in a
  [Yahoo News expert column, 9 Aug 2025](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/323d7a920a40e10ca9875c5bb24799382d25550c).
  Complaint: under the old system a leftover day could be burned on a short hop;
  consecutive days make it much harder to break even, *"particularly when you hit a
  service disruption mid-trip."*
- Railfan blogger アルファ模型 calls it **改悪** (a degradation).
  [note](https://note.com/alpha923/n/nd20c8f9d033a)
- ⚠️ **Shikoku-specific warning:** with consecutive days you must cover more ground
  per day, which collides badly with Shikoku's sparse timetables.
  [Shikoku Rail Note, upd. 13 Dec 2025](https://shikoku-railway-note.com/takamatsu-matsuyama-futsu-densha/)

## 2. ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass — ⚠️ major changes, act on this

### Current price (until 30 Sep 2026)

| Days | Bought overseas | Bought in Japan / WEST-TR |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | ¥12,000 | ¥12,500 |
| 4 | ¥15,000 | ¥15,500 |
| 5 | ¥17,000 | ¥17,500 |
| 7 | ¥20,000 | ¥20,500 |

Children 6–11 half price.
[SHIKOKU Railway Trip official](https://shikoku-railwaytrip.com/pass-information/)

### ⚠️ CHANGE 1 — Iyotetsu removed, effective 7 March 2026
JR Shikoku press release 31 Oct 2025: 「２０２６年３月７日以降、伊予鉄道をご利用できません」
(passes spanning 7 Mar stay valid on Iyotetsu through 6 Mar). No price change.
[EN notice PDF](https://www.jr-shikoku.co.jp/global/en/news/pdf/20251031-all-shikoku-rail-pass.pdf)

### ⚠️ CHANGE 2 — product overhaul effective 1 October 2026
JR Shikoku press release **6 Aug 2026**
([PDF](https://www.jr-shikoku.co.jp/03_news/press/assets/2026/08/06/20260806.pdf)):
- **4-day and 5-day passes discontinued.** Only **3-day and 7-day** remain.
- **Domestic / WEST-TR prices cut** to match overseas: 3-day →**¥12,000**,
  7-day →**¥20,000**.
- **Shodoshima Ferry and Shodoshima Olive Bus dropped.** Passes bought on/before
  30 Sep 2026 keep them.
- **KAGAWA Mini Rail & Ferry Pass discontinued** from 30 Sep 2026.
- **Trip.com QR ticketing added** from 1 Oct 2026 (Klook QR live since Apr 2025).

### Coverage
- JR Shikoku **and Tosa Kuroshio Railway** all lines: **limited express ordinary-car
  NON-RESERVED seats** ✅ included, plus rapid and local non-reserved.
- **Asa Kaigan Railway** (incl. DMV), **Kotoden**, **Tosaden Kotsu** — all rail/tram.
- **Iyotetsu** — only until 6 Mar 2026.
- **Shodoshima Ferry (Takamatsu–Tonoshō only) + Olive Bus** — only on passes sold
  to 30 Sep 2026.
- **Excluded:** Sunrise Seto, Botchan Train, **all bus routes**
  (「各社のバス路線には乗車できません」), green car, high-speed ferries, any other
  ferry (so **not** Naoshima).
- Honshu side: valid as far as **Kojima**.
- Seat reservations: **¥1,200 for four**.

### Eligibility
Foreign passport + **Temporary Visitor** status only, non-transferable. Overseas via
agents and **Klook / Trip.com (QR)**; in Japan at Takamatsu, Matsuyama, Tokushima,
Kōchi, Kotohira stations, from 1 month before.

### Is it worth it?
- **japan-guide:** *"The 3-day pass will still likely pay off if visiting at least
  two cities on the island."*
- **Break-even against live fares (Ekitan, 16 Aug 2026):** Takamatsu→Matsuyama by
  特急 non-reserved = **¥6,160**; Kōchi→Takamatsu = **¥5,050**. So **two long
  limited-express legs already exceed the ¥12,000 3-day pass.**
- ⚠️ **Honest counterpoint for OUR travellers:** riding only local trains costs
  **¥3,960** Takamatsu→Matsuyama and **¥3,190** Takamatsu→Kōchi. **On a
  locals-only itinerary the pass rarely pays** — it pays when you *stop* avoiding
  limited expresses. Also: **Iya Valley buses are not covered.**

## 3. Other passes

### JR West Setouchi Area Pass
**¥22,000 adult / ¥11,000 child, 7 consecutive days.** Foreign passport +
Temporary Visitor. Covers Sanyō Shinkansen Shin-Osaka⇄Hakata (non-reserved),
LEX **HARUKA, SHIOKAZE, NANPU**, and Special Rapid/Rapid/Local on **JR West AND
JR Shikoku**; plus JR-West Miyajima Ferry, Shodoshima Ferry, Ishizaki Kisen,
**Setonaikai Kisen** (the Matsuyama–Hiroshima boats), Okayama Electric Tramway,
Hiroshima Meipuru-pu bus.
⚠️ Official EN page is stamped **"current as of 1 August 2024"** and the Shikoku
free-area boundary is shown **only as a map image**. **Treat Kōchi coverage as
unconfirmed.** [EN](http://www.westjr.co.jp/travel-information/en/tickets-passes/other/setouchi/)

## 4. Local-train realities — the sparse-service truth

Baseline: JR Shikoku's **14 March 2026 timetable revision**, read off Yahoo!路線情報
weekday station timetables and Ekitan.

### Trains per day and the killer gaps

| Line / section | Locals/day | Biggest gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Yosan**: Kannonji→Matsuyama/Iyo-Saijō | 18 | 1h24 |
| **Yosan**: Yawatahama→Uwajima | 8 | **4h14** (8:20→12:34) |
| **Yosan**: Uwajima→Matsuyama dir. | 7 | **5h24** (6:47→12:11) |
| Yosan coastal old line at Shimonada | 8–9 | 2h44 |
| **Dosan**: Awa-Ikeda→Kōchi | **only 4 get past Ōboke** | **6h04** (7:45→13:49) |
| **Dosan**: Ōboke→Kōchi dir. | **4** | **6h07** |
| **Dosan**: Susaki→Kubokawa | 5 | **6h20** |
| **Tokushima**: Anabuki→Awa-Ikeda | 10 | 4h10 |
| **Mugi**: Awa-Kainan→Anan | 10, **3 of them 代行バス** | 3h18 |
| **Yodo**: Kubokawa→Uwajima | **4 dep., only 3 run the full line** | — |
| **Yodo**: Uwajima→Kubokawa | 9 dep. but **only 2 guaranteed through-runs** | — |

⚠️ Wikipedia's "Yodo Line = 4 round trips" is **stale** — live 2026 data is
3 down / 2–3 up. Yodo Line ridership: **150 passengers/day (FY2023)**, down from
575 in FY1989.
⚠️ **No local train runs Takamatsu→Uwajima through** — services split at
Kannonji / Iyo-Saijō / Matsuyama.

### (a) Takamatsu → Kōchi all-local — **YES, but you can't start early**

| | Depart | Arrive | Time | Fare | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Best | **10:52** | **16:44** | **5h52** | **¥3,190** | Tadotsu, Awa-Ikeda (1h01 wait) |
| Cheapest | 10:20 | 16:44 | 6h24 | ¥3,040 | via Kotoden (⚠️ **not** covered by Seishun 18) |
| Latest | 15:13 | 20:13 | 5h00 | ¥3,190 | Tadotsu, Awa-Ikeda, Tosa-Yamada |

A 06:00 start **does not help** — a local from Takamatsu cannot reach Awa-Ikeda
before the 7:45 Kōchi train leaves. **Reverse is worse:** Kōchi 06:27→Takamatsu
15:33 = **9h06**.

Real account (Mar 2024): did it all-local via Tadotsu and Awa-Ikeda with a 1-hour
lunch stop, ~4h riding, *"less tiring than expected"*; highlights were the
**Tsubojiri and Shingai switchback stations**.
[アジアぶらぶら旅日記](https://asiannight410.blog.fc2.com/blog-entry-1835.html)

### (b) Tokushima → Kōchi all-local — **YES, take the afternoon train**

| | Depart | Arrive | Time | Fare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Earliest arrival | 07:35 | 16:44 | **9h09** (only 5h05 riding — **~4h dead at Awa-Ikeda**) | ¥3,630 |
| **Recommended** | **14:52** | **20:13** | **5h21** | ¥3,630 |

Real account (Aug 2022): Tokushima 11:38 → Awa-Ikeda 13:47, then **a 2-minute
connection** onto 4237D → Kōchi 16:06.
「これを逃すと３時間待たねばならなくなるので選択肢はありません」 — miss it and you wait
3 hours. [DRFC-OB](https://drfc-ob.com/wp/archives/135340)

### (c) Kōchi → Matsuyama — **the notorious one**

**All-local rail: technically possible, practically no.** Fastest is **9h58,
¥5,380, 3 changes**, and it's a single daily thread:
Kōchi **05:39** → Kubokawa 07:59 → *wait 1h40* → Kubokawa **09:39** (Yodo Line)
→ Kita-Uwajima 12:03 → Iyo-Ōzu 14:04 → Matsuyama **15:37**.
**Miss the 05:39 and you cannot arrive same-day.**

**What people actually do: the bus.**

| Service | Operators | Time | Freq | Fare |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **なんごくエクスプレス** | JR Shikoku Bus | ~3h00 | **5 round trips** (Kōchi dep 8:30, 10:20, 13:20, 16:30, 18:20) | **¥4,000**; return ¥7,200; 得特1 ¥3,000; **¥3,000 showing a Seishun 18**; web 10-ride ¥2,000/ride |
| **ホエールエクスプレス** | Iyotetsu + Tosaden | **2h33–2h42** | **4 round trips** | fare **not stated in HTML** (PDF only) |

The bus is **~7½ hours faster and cheaper** than all-local rail. A March-2026 ride
report: faster and more economical than rail, no transfer, less shaking, no tunnel
signal dropouts. [バスレポ](https://secure.j-bus.co.jp/busrepo/2026/04/09/41793/)

⚠️ The **ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass covers no buses** — but there's a separate
**ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass専用乗車券 at ¥3,000** for the Nangoku Express, sold only at
JR Shikoku Bus plazas on showing the pass.

### (d) Takamatsu → Matsuyama all-local
**4.5–5 hours, ¥3,960, two changes (Kannonji and Iyo-Saijō)**, vs LEX ¥6,160/2h34
and bus ¥4,400/2h50.
⚠️ **IC cards only work in the Takamatsu area** — buy a paper ticket or you cannot
exit at Matsuyama. Connection waits run 30 min to over an hour.

## 7. Practical bottom line

| Crossing | All-local verdict | Realistic figure | Better option |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Takamatsu → Kōchi | **Doable**, only from ~10:52 | 5h52, ¥3,190 | Bus ¥3,900 / 2h20 |
| Kōchi → Takamatsu | Painful | 9h06 (06:27) or 6h08 (12:45) | Bus |
| Tokushima → Kōchi | **Doable** from 14:52 | 5h21, ¥3,630 | Bus ¥4,000 / 2h50 (fare pre-2024) |
| Takamatsu → Matsuyama | Doable | 4.5–5h, ¥3,960, 2 changes | Bus ¥4,400 / 2h50 |
| **Kōchi → Matsuyama** | **No** | 9h58, ¥5,380, one daily thread | **Bus ¥4,000 — or ¥3,000 showing a Seishun 18** |
| Yodo Line | 3 through trains/day west, 2 east | Budget a full day | **It's a destination, not a connection** |

**Three things worth acting on now:**
1. The ALL SHIKOKU Rail Pass loses its 4- and 5-day options and Shodoshima on
   **1 Oct 2026**.
2. Iyotetsu already left the pass on **7 Mar 2026**, and the ALL IYOTETSU pass has
   been dead since **Feb 2024** — both still wrong in most published guides.
3. The **Seishun 18 → ¥3,000 Nangoku Express** discount is the clean answer to the
   Kōchi–Matsuyama problem, documented on JR Shikoku Bus's own page.
