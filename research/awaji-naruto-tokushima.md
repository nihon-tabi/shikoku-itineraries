# Osaka → Awaji Island → Naruto whirlpools → Tokushima, carless — sourced research

**The one structural fact everything follows from: Awaji has no railway.** Every
movement on the island is a highway bus (fast, frequent, expensive) or a
local/community bus (cheap, sparse, hub-and-spoke around Sumoto Bus Center).
The single bus line crossing the Ōnaruto Bridge for ordinary passengers is Awaji
Kōtsū's **淡路・徳島線 — 4 runs/day weekdays, 3 on weekends.**

⚠️ **Time-critical:** Nankai Ferry has announced **route withdrawal targeting end
of March 2028** (possibly earlier), and **since 2026-08-01 was cut from 8 to 6
round trips/day** (crew shortage, 当面の間).
<https://nankai-ferry.co.jp/3831/> · [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUF222DJ0S6A720C2000000/)

## 3. Naruto whirlpools — the tide problem that makes or breaks the day

### ⚠️ Read this before booking anything
Whirlpools are a tidal-current phenomenon; peaks come roughly every ~6 hours, so
most days have one morning and one afternoon window.

**Official calendars:**
- **渦の道 tide calendar — <https://www.uzunomichi.jp/tide-calendar/>** — Aug 2026
  through late 2027. **Red = 大潮 spring tide, blue = 中潮, black = 小潮.** The
  homepage also shows 「本日のうずしおベストタイム」.
- **うずしお観潮船 tide table — <https://www.uzusio.com/en/siomi/>** (EN), Jan 2026–Dec 2027
- **うずしお汽船 潮見表 — <https://www.uzushio-kisen.com/shiomihyou.html>**
- **Fukura-side cruise timetable with per-sailing 期待度 (high/med/low) —
  <https://www.uzu-shio.com/timetable>**

**⭐ Key correction to the widely-repeated "±1.5 hours" rule — the window scales
with tide strength:**
> 「観潮には、この時刻を中心に、**大潮は前後２時間、中潮は前後１時間半、小潮は前後１時間まで**
> が最適です。」

**大潮** = the days around new/full moon, *"when the difference between the high and
low tides is greatest… the largest whirlpool is expected."*

Operator's own disclaimer:
> 「渦潮は潮の干満の差によって生ずる自然現象です。**時間によっては渦潮の巻かない時間帯もあり、
> その時間帯は毎日変化します。**」

**Real travellers who got it wrong:**
- Blog literally titled *"Naruto Whirlpool - Visiting at the wrong timing"*:
  *"It was late afternoon and we had missed the best timing, which is supposed to
  be from 11am to 1pm."* … *"All we saw was the overlapping of different waters."*
  Rain also fogged the Uzu-no-michi glass.
  <https://travelfeed.com/@jrvacation/narutowhirlpool-visitingatthewrongtiming-ft6vzr6hne>
- TripAdvisor review titled **"No whirlpool at non-peak hours"**
- **Expectation management even at peak:** reviewers report *"a few small swirls and
  a larger area of turbulent water"*, nothing like the brochure.
- ⚠️ **The carless trap:** one reviewer notes that if peak tide lands at ~09:30 with
  sailings at 09:00 and 09:40, there may be **no bus that gets you there in time**.
  Cross-check the tide window against the bus timetable **before fixing your date**.
  Earliest possible arrival at 鳴門公園 is **07:40** weekdays.

### ⭐ CAN you get off mid-bridge at Naruto Park? Yes — but only one way.

**Awaji Kōtsū's own fare table warns:**
> 「**鳴門公園口バス停は特殊なバス停です。** このバス停は本四道路上にあり、「**徳島方面行き**」
> バス停はありますが、「**淡路方面行き**」バス停はありませんのでご注意ください。」

**And** <https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/鳴門公園口バスストップ>:
> 「鳴門方面へ向かう車線にのみバス停が設置されている為、京阪神方面へ向う高速バスは停車しない」
> 「停車する高速バスは全て鳴門・徳島行であり、**クローズドドアシステムではない淡路交通の
> 淡路・徳島線を除いては全便降車のみの扱い**となっている」

**Practical:** riding south from Awaji (or from Kobe/Osaka/Kyoto on the 阿波
エクスプレス), you **can alight at 鳴門公園口** and walk to the whirlpools, skipping
Naruto Station entirely. You generally **cannot board** there northbound; the sole
exception is Awaji Kōtsū's 淡路・徳島線.
Walking distance 鳴門公園口 → 渦の道: **not stated** (the tourism association's
dedicated PDF now 404s); a nearby hotel states 「バス停「鳴門公園口」から徒歩約10分」
to itself, past エディ and 渦の道.

### ⭐ The plain alternative: Nankai Ferry, Wakayama → Tokushima
<https://nankai-ferry.co.jp/> · fares <https://nankai-ferry.co.jp/price/>

- **Adult foot passenger ¥2,500 one-way**, round trip ¥4,880, child ¥1,250;
  **bicycle ¥2,600** on top. Fuel surcharge ¥0 for passengers.
  Valid 2025-10-01 – 2026-12-31.
- **Crossing about 2h05–2h20** per the published timetable (company says 約2時間15分).
- ⚠️ **Since 1 Aug 2026 cut from 8 to 6 round trips/day.** Suspended: Wakayama dep.

- ⚠️ **Route withdrawal targeted for end of March 2028.**

**🔥 The killer deal — とくしま好きっぷ ¥2,500:** Nankai train from *any* Nankai
station (Namba, Shin-Imamiya, Kansai Airport…) to Wakayamako **+** the ferry to
Tokushima, for the **same ¥2,500 as the ferry alone. The train leg is effectively
free.** <https://www.nankai.co.jp/traffic/otoku/skip2000.html> (¥2,500 adult /
¥1,250 child since 1 Oct 2023). Reverse direction ¥2,500 from Tokushima.
⚠️ **Window sales only** — 「「好きっぷ」はWEB予約できません（窓口発売のみ）」.

**Reaching Wakayamako without express fees:** Namba → Wakayamashi (Nankai Main
Line) → Wakayamako. Fare **¥970** per Ekitan (Nankai publishes no static fare page —
treat as third-party). **No express fee is required even on the Sazan limited

ご乗車頂けます**」 (reserved seat ¥700). The Wakayamako Line is timed to the ferry —
Nankai Ferry's timetable page prints the connecting train times against each sailing.
**JR Pass / Seishun 18: no** — Nankai is a private railway.

**Tokushima-ko → Tokushima Station:** city bus route 4 「沖洲・南海フェリー線」, stop
南海フェリー前. **¥250** (Tokushima city flat fare since 1 Oct 2024). **About 25 min.**
⚠️ 「**フェリーとバスの連絡はしておりませんのでご注意下さい**」 — the buses are *not*
timed to sailings. (One blogger walked 45 min to Awa-Tomida Station instead.)

## 7. Suggested carless shape (entirely from the sources above)

1. **Pick the date from <https://www.uzunomichi.jp/tide-calendar/> FIRST** — find a
   red 大潮 day, note the peak time, check it against the bus tables. Everything
   else bends around this.
2. **Osaka → Awaji, cheapest:** JR to 舞子 → walk to 高速舞子 → bus **¥600** to 淡路IC.
   Or 明石 → 岩屋 by Jenova Line, **¥700, 13 min** — nicest arrival, and Iwaya has
   the ¥880 day-pass counter (Information Center Bird, Iwaya Port Terminal 1F).
3. **On Awaji, pick ONE corridor.** North-west (free Pasona shuttle + ¥1,000
   あわ神あわ姫 day pass) **or** Sumoto-as-hub (¥880 Awaji Kōtsū day pass, 縦貫線
   spine). Plan on <https://busmo.656.ch/>. **Carry cash — island buses don't take
   IC cards** (PayPay reportedly works).
4. **Sleep** at Awaji Tourist Trophy House (100 m from Goshiki Bus Center, on the
   ¥880 pass) or Guesthouse Shimauta in Shizuki — **confirm dorm availability**,
   since Shimauta's dorm plans are currently not bookable.
5. **Cross to Naruto** on the 淡路・徳島線: weekday 洲本BC 6:55 / 9:00 / 11:15 / 16:00,
   or from Fukura via 淡路島南IC at 7:20 / 11:25 / 16:25. **Alight at 鳴門公園口
   (¥1,380 from Sumoto BC)** — remember you cannot board there to go back.
6. **Whirlpools:** 渦の道 ¥510 as the cheap fallback; Wonder Naruto ¥2,000 or
   Uzushio Kisen ¥2,000 (**cash only**) walk-up; Aqua Eddy ¥2,500 **by reservation
   only**.
7. **On to Tokushima** on 徳島バス from 鳴門公園 (~¥390 to 鳴門駅前, third-party), or
   stay on the Awaji bus to 徳島 (¥2,120 from Sumoto BC). Sleep at **HOSTEL PAQ**,
   8 minutes' walk from the station.
8. **Return to Osaka cheaply:** **とくしま好きっぷ ¥2,500** — window sales only, and
   note the reduced 6-sailings-a-day schedule and the 2028 withdrawal.
