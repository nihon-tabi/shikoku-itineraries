## Sourcing rules

Tier every figure, and say so: **operator** (the company that charges it) >
**official** (a city or tourism board) > **third-party** > **traveller**.

Never estimate a travel time or a price. If a figure is not published, write
"not stated" and say who to ring — do not fill the gap with a plausible number.
