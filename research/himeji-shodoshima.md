## 1. Osaka → Himeji on a non-express train

### ⭐ JR 新快速 Shin-Kaisoku (Special Rapid) — the answer

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Surcharge** | **NONE.** 「普通運賃（乗車券）のみで乗ることができる」 — rideable on the base fare ticket alone |
| Journey time | **1 h 03–06 min** Osaka → Himeji |
| **Fare** | **¥1,460** adult one-way (¥2,920 return), 87.9 km |
| Frequency | **every 15 min** daytime |
| Stops | Amagasaki, Ashiya, Sannomiya, Kobe, Akashi, Nishi-Akashi, Kakogawa, Himeji |

<https://kansai-norikae.com/shinkaisoku/> · <https://ekitan.com/transit/fare/sf-5006/st-5884>

**Proof it's surcharge-free:** the same fare engine prices the Shinkansen routing as
"base fare ¥1,460 + express surcharge ¥1,760 = ¥3,220". **¥1,460 is the universal
base fare; Shin-Kaisoku adds nothing.**
*(Optional 「Aシート」 reserved car is ¥600–860 extra — skip it.)*
⚠️ I could not load a JR West page stating the fare directly (their shinkaisoku page
404s). The no-surcharge fact is solid; the exact ¥1,460 is from a fare database.

### Sanyo/Hanshin 直通特急 — cheaper, much slower, not worth it
**¥1,350, 1 h 44 min, no transfers, no surcharge** despite the "特急" name
(<https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/直通特急_(阪神・山陽)>).
**Saves ¥110, costs 38 extra minutes**, and runs Hanshin Osaka-Umeda → **Sanyo-Himeji**
(a different station from JR Himeji, roughly the same distance from the castle).
**Take the Shin-Kaisoku.**

## 2. Himeji Castle

### ⚠️⚠️ Admission nearly TRIPLED on 1 March 2026 — this is already in effect

**Residency-based, not nationality-based.**

| Category | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| **Adult 18+, non-resident** | **¥2,500** (was ¥1,000) |
| Adult 18+, Himeji resident | ¥1,000 |
| **Anyone under 18** | **FREE** (was ¥300) |
| **Castle + Koko-en combo** | **¥2,600** |
| Koko-en alone | ¥400 |

<https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/shisei/0000032640.html> (「令和8年3月1日より…料金を改定します」)
· <https://visit-himeji.com/en/news/revision-of-himeji-castle-admission-fees/>

**The combo saves only ¥300** (¥2,500 + ¥400 = ¥2,900 vs ¥2,600).
⚠️ **Most blogs still quote the old ¥1,050 combo** — e.g. travelwithapen.com. Budget
**¥2,600 per person**. For two backpackers that is **¥5,200** — a serious line item,
and worth deciding deliberately rather than by default.

Hours **09:00–17:00, last entry 16:00**, closed 29–30 Dec.

### Walk from the station
- **Official castle site: "20-minute walk from JR Himeji and Sanyo Himeji stations."**
  Visit Himeji says 15 minutes — the two city-run sources disagree.
- *"When you leave the station using the North Exit, you can already see Himeji castle!
  No need to worry about getting lost!"* — [Bassett's Bouken](https://bassettsbouken.com/west-japan-in-a-week-itinerary/);
  taxi *"under ¥1000"*.
- Bus alternative (official): Shinki bus from the North Exit to **Otemon-mae**, then 5 min walk.

### How long?
Hard constraint, official: **「姫路城大天守への登城者数は1時間あたり1,000人としております」** —
the keep is capped at **1,000 entries/hour**, and queues form on busy days.
<https://www.city.himeji.lg.jp/castle/0000007671.html>

- Bassett's Bouken: *"Exploring Himeji castle took me about an hour and a half… you
  could easily enjoy 2-3 hours"* + *"at least 45 minutes for Kokoen."*
- Earth Trekkers: *"TIME NEEDED | 2 to 4 hours."*
- Reddit snippet: *"We went at 1030am ish on a week day… and there was no queue."*

⚠️ **The keep climb is not trivial** — official: *"It's like climbing a small mountain
to get to the main keep."* Earth Trekkers: *"you will climb up all six levels. The
staircases are very steep… Shoes must be removed. Wear thick, comfortable socks."*
**Relevant to the no-hiking constraint: the grounds and Koko-en are flat and lovely;
the keep interior is six floors of steep ladder-stairs in socks.** Skipping the keep
interior is a legitimate choice.

⚠️ Peak (sakura late Mar–early Apr, Golden Week, summer) adds **2–3 hours of queueing**.
**Live wait times are published at the bottom of <https://www.himejicastle.jp/>** —
check the morning of.

### Luggage — solved
**JR Himeji Station, North Exit: Small ¥400 (63 units), Medium ¥500 (87), Large ¥700
(64) — IC card or cash, 24-hour access.** Best bank: biggest, IC payment, always open.
Other banks (Shinkansen gate, Piole South) are **cash only** with limited hours.
<https://realtrip-himeji.com/usefulinformation/156908/>
Manned storage: MONZEN ¥1,000/bag 09:00–18:00; Clean Market ¥500–800; Yamato Yashiki
¥500/bag, card OK.

## 3. Himeji → Shodoshima ferry (Himeji Port → Fukuda Port)

### ✅ Operating normally (operator status board shows 平常運航)
**Shodoshima Ferry / 四国フェリーグループ** — <https://www.shikokuferry.com/route1>

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Crossing | **~100 minutes**, 41 km |
| **Adult one-way** | **¥2,100** (return ¥3,990); child ¥1,050 |
| **Sailings** | **7 each way, daily** |
| **Himeji dep.** | **07:15 · 09:45 · 11:15 · 13:35 · 15:10 · 17:25 · 19:30** |
| **Fukuda dep.** | **07:50 · 09:20 · 11:40 · 13:15 · 15:30 · 17:15 · 19:30** |
| Bicycle | +¥2,720 |
| Reservations | **Vehicles only.** Foot passengers buy at the window on the day. |
| Payment | cash, card, e-money; **no QR** |

⚠️ **Fare-inflation warning — almost every secondary source is stale:** japan-guide
still says ¥1,900; 小豆島BASE and a 2023 ride report say ¥1,710; a 2020 report says
¥1,550. **Budget ¥2,100.** The operator's page is the authority.

### Himeji Station → Himeji Port (Shinki Bus)
- **Route 94**, from **Platform 1 (1番のりば), Himeji Station North Exit**
  *(platform number is well-corroborated by two independent travellers but I found no
  official Shinki page stating it)*
- **Journey ~20 min** (route engines + a 2017 trip report) / **~30 min** per the
  official Ieshima tourism site
- **Fare ¥320** per NAVITIME/Jorudan — ⚠️ **no official Shinki page states it**;
  a ¥280 figure was current in 2022 and shodoshima.com still quotes it
- **~2 per hour**; first 06:20, last 21:00 station→port
- Returning, buses arrive at Himeji Station **stop 12**
- Taxi alternative ~15 min, ~¥2,500

> **Budget 40 minutes station-to-gate.** Route 94 runs every ~30 min so a missed bus
> isn't fatal — but a missed ferry costs 1.5–2.5 hours.

### ⚠️ Onward from Fukuda Port — this is the weak link
**Two Olive Bus routes serve Fukuda**; the useful one is **南廻り福田線 (South Fukuda
Line): Tonosho Port ↔ Fukuda Port** via Olive Town, Ikeda Port, Kusakabe Port.
- **~27 km, ~1 hour, ¥500** (the maximum single fare on the island)
- **⚠️ 「乗り換えは5分！超タイト」** — the ferry/bus connection at Fukuda is a
  **5-minute transfer**
- **⚠️ There is no taxi company at Fukuda Port** — 「福田港にタクシー会社はありません。
  乗船前に連絡を」
- **Number of daily runs: not stated** — operator publishes only CID-encoded PDFs.
  **Get the printed timetable at the port.**
- Worked examples from real travellers: the **09:45 Himeji ferry** connects to the
  **11:45 Fukuda→Tonosho bus**; a 2023 day-tripper took the 09:30 ferry → Fukuda
  ~11:00 → 20–30 min bus to Olive Park.

## 4. Shodoshima without a car

### 4a. Olive Bus — ⚠️ fares and passes rose 1 April 2025

| Product | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **1-day pass** | **¥1,600 adult / ¥800 child** (was ¥1,000/¥500) |
| 1-day pass during **Setouchi Triennale** | **¥2,000 / ¥1,000** |
| Single fares | zones ¥200 / ¥300 / ¥400 / **¥500 max** |

<https://www.shodoshima-olive-bus.com/ticket/>
⚠️ **2-day and 3-day passes are NOT on the operator's ticket page — treat as
discontinued.** Aggregators still show a ¥1,500 "2-day" pass; that's pre-April-2025.
**Payment: cash and IC cards (Suica/PASMO). No QR, no e-money.**
**Break-even: two ¥500 rides plus anything else beats ¥1,600** — so if you land at
Fukuda and cross the island, buy the day pass.

Nine routes. The trunk **Sakate Line** (Tonosho↔Sakate) is ~1/hour; the
**Nakayama, Nishiura and Movie Village lines run about 5 times a day.**

### 4b. ⭐ Bicycles — the real workaround for bus gaps
**HELLO CYCLING share-cycling, electric-assist, ¥200/15 min or ¥2,000/12 h**, 24 h,
app required, **return to any station**. Stations at Tonosho Port, Kusakabe Port (×2),
inside Olive Park. <https://shodoshima.com/info/bicycle/>
Also: Tonosho Port Kanko Center regular bicycles **¥500/day**; Ikeda Port e-bike
¥1,500/4 h or ¥2,000/day.

**Real advice:** 「土庄港からバスで草壁港またはオリーブ公園口まで行き、電動シェアサイクルを
借りて紅雲亭まで行くのが現実的」 — bus first, *then* share-cycle; riding from Tonosho
Port is 「かなり大変」.
⚠️ **Counterpoint:** 「オリーブ公園への坂は、電動でも厳しいくらいの急坂」 — even an
e-bike struggles on the Olive Park climb. **Given the no-hiking preference, treat
bikes as a gap-filler on the flat coast road, not as the plan.**

### 4c. Sight by sight

#### ⚠️ Kankakei Gorge ropeway 寒霞渓 — the bus arrangement CHANGED in March 2025
From **20 March 2025** the Kankakei bus became a **town-operated shuttle**; Olive Bus
runs its own Kankakei service for **only 10 days, 21–30 November**.
<https://shodoshima.or.jp/news/detail.php?id=591>

- **Route: Kusakabe Port 草壁港 → Kouuntei 紅雲亭 (ropeway lower station).**
  Nonstop, **~11 min**, 29-seat microbus, **8 runs/day**.
- **Fare: FREE** — but explicitly trial: 「現在は試験的に無料で運行していますが…将来的には
  有料化を予定しています」
- **From Tonosho Port:** Sakate Line or South Fukuda Line to **Kusakabe Port (~30 min)**,
  then the shuttle — **~1 hour total**.

**Ropeway fares** (<https://www.kankakei.co.jp/ropeway/>), adult **round trip / one way**:
**21 Mar–31 Oct & 1–10 Dec ¥2,340 / ¥1,300** · **1–30 Nov (autumn peak) ¥2,700 /
¥1,500** · **11 Dec–20 Mar ¥2,160 / ¥1,200**. Children 50%. Every 12 min.
Hours 08:30–17:00 (08:00–17:00 during 21 Oct–30 Nov; 08:30–16:30 winter).

**Worked car-free connection (real plan):** arrive Kusakabe Port 10:11 → **free shuttle
10:30** → Kouuntei 10:41 → ropeway 10:47; down 12:12 → shuttle 12:30 → Kusakabe 12:41.
**Real report, April weekday:** 「人員不足で路線バスは走っていないようで」 — only the
8-a-day shuttle; 「シャトルバスの乗客は行きも帰りも、私ひとりだけでした」 (sole passenger
both ways). Walked back down in ~40 min.
⚠️ **japan-guide describes the PRE-2025 arrangement** (every 2 hours, weekend-only in
shoulder seasons, suspended mid-Dec to mid-Mar). **Trust the town's current calendar.**

#### ⭐ Angel Road エンジェルロード — the one sight needing zero buses
- **Just walk: ~30 min from Tonosho Port** (official). Bus alternative is the Nishiura
  Line, ~10 min, alight 「国際ホテル（エンジェルロード前）」 + 5 min walk — but with
  **only 5 runs a day, walking is often faster.**
- **Tides:** official table at <https://www.shikokuferry.com/angel> — **two windows a
  day, each ~6 hours** (sample day 04:40–10:40 and 16:36–22:36). Rule of thumb: ±2 h
  around low tide. Visitor centre 09:00–16:30.

#### ⭐ Shodoshima Olive Park オリーブ公園 — the easiest sight, and flat
Alight **「サン・オリーブ」 ≈0 min walk** or **「オリーブ公園口」 ≈5 min walk**.
Served by **Sakate Line, South Fukuda Line and Tanoura Line** — **reachable in one bus
from every ferry port.** Open **08:30–17:00 daily, year-round.**
Has a **HELLO CYCLING station inside the park.**

#### Nakayama rice terraces 中山千枚田 — only 5 buses a day
Nakayama Line loop from Tonosho Port, alight **「中山春日神社前」**.
**Tonosho Port dep. 7:30, 9:00, 11:55, 15:10, 17:20**; Ikeda Port dep. 8:06, 9:40,
12:35, 15:46, 18:00. **~20 min / ¥300 from Tonosho.**
⚠️ **Missing one bus costs ~3 hours.**

#### ❌ Choshikei Monkey Park — NOT reachable by public bus
The operator says so on its own access page:
> 「※銚子渓・お猿の国までの小豆島オリーブバスの運行はありませんので、自動車・バイク・
> 観光ツアーでのご来園をオススメします。」
> — <http://www.osaru-no-kuni.sakura.ne.jp/access.html>

**Only public option: the reserved sightseeing tour bus** — Tonosho Port **10:15,
back 16:15 (~6 h), ¥9,800 adult**, includes guide, two admissions and a one-way
Kankakei ropeway. Reservation required, 0879-62-1203. **Too expensive for this trip.**

#### Twenty-Four Eyes Movie Village 二十四の瞳映画村
Tanoura Line from Tonosho, or **Sakate Line ~13 min from Sakate Port**.
**¥750 adult**, 09:00–17:00 year-round. ⚠️ 「日に5本くらい」 — about 5 buses a day.

#### Soy sauce area 醤の郷
**Marukin Soy Sauce Museum** — **Sakate Line, alight 「丸金前」, immediately outside.**
**¥500 adult**, 09:00–16:00.
**Yamaroku Soy Sauce** — **free tour, no reservation, 09:00–17:00 daily.** Tonosho Port
→ 「安田上」 42 min then 10 min walk; Sakate Port → 「安田」 8 min.
⚠️ Its own page warns **buses come "only once every few hours."**
⚠️ 「醤の郷エリア: 16:00〜17:00頃に多くの施設が閉店するため時間厳禁」

### 4e. Is a car essential? No — but plan around the buses

**japan-guide is blunt:** *"The most convenient means to get around Shodoshima is a
rental car."* Buses ~2/hour along the south coast, "considerably less frequent
elsewhere."

**But car-free travellers say it works:**
- Reddit (snippet): *"We did a day trip out from takamatsu: ferry from takamatsu to
  tonosho, and worked our way by bus and walking over to Sakate and took the jumbo
  ferry from Sakate back to Takamatsu. **Easy and reliable bus system, just infrequent
  so plan accordingly.**"*
- Yahoo!知恵袋: 「エンジェルロード、オリーブ園、ふるさと村、そうめん、醤油くらいなら
  バスでも大丈夫」 — those are fine by bus; **Kankakei is the seasonal problem.**
- Donny Kimball's sharpest line: *"there are better and more accessible ways to spend
  your time than trying to transfer to a super infrequent bus line."*

⚠️ **Setouchi Triennale crowding — strongest first-person evidence.** A journalist
toured Shodoshima entirely by public transport during Triennale 2025 and titled the
piece 「…ただしバスにはリスクもありました」:
> 「公共交通機関はレンタカーの使えない外国人が多く、**相当混雑するケースがあるリスクを
> 覚悟する必要があります**」
> 「地元の高校生も立っていて、自分が観光公害に加担していると思うと切ない気分でした」

He described the **16:28 return as 「すし詰め状態」** (packed like sushi).
<https://artexhibition.jp/topics/news/20250501-AEJ2640470/>

> **Budget 2 nights on Shodoshima** if you want Kankakei + Angel Road + the soy sauce
> area car-free. **One night forces you to pick two of three.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Sakate Port → Takamatsu — the biggest car-free trap on this whole route
**Jumbo Ferry**, adult **¥700**, ~1 h 15 min, 3/day weekdays.
> 「ジャンボフェリーの高松のりばは、高松駅前の「高松港」ではなく、車で約15分離れた
> **「高松東港」**です」

**It docks at Takamatsu-HIGASHI Port, ~15 min away from the walkable Takamatsu Port**,
reachable only by a free shuttle timed to sailings.
**Booking a bed by the wrong port is the most common mistake in this area.**
<https://ferry.co.jp/home/ports-all/takamatsu/>

- **For ferry access:** the **JR Takamatsu Station / Chikko** cluster. Among budget

- **For the cheapest real dorm beds, plus food and nightlife: Kawaramachi (瓦町)** —
  WeBase at ¥3,500 (¥2,600 weekly) leads. Kawaramachi is **one Kotoden stop /
  ~10–15 min walk** from Chikko, so the ferry penalty is minor.
- **Avoid** Nakanocho/Ritsurin and Kanko-cho if the itinerary is driven by early ferries.
