### 1c. Booking
- **No online purchase, therefore no online discount.** Official EN: *"Please
  pay at the ticket counter on the day of passage. Payments cannot be made in
  advance."* Email holds a slot only, until 17:00 (jet) / 18:00 (ferry) the day before.
- Foot passengers on the Cruise Ferry **cannot reserve at all** — buy at the
  window 20 min before.
- Check-in opens **50 min** before Linear Jet departure. Baggage ≤2 m, ≤30 kg,
  2 pieces free.
- **JR West Setouchi Area Pass covers this crossing, including the fast boat.**
  JR West's written reply, quoted by "Kim (guest)" 2024-09-01: *"It is possible
  to use this pass to board the SuperJet on the Hiroshima Port – Kure –
  Matsuyama Kanko Port route… All seats are unreserved."*
  <https://www.japan-guide.com/forum/quereadisplay.html?0+181853>
- ⚠️ **Foreign-visitor discount is likely expired.** The Setouchi board still
  shows passport fares (¥5,600 jet / ¥3,500 ferry) but states validity
  **1 Apr 2024 – 31 Mar 2025**, and it's absent from the operator's current
  discount section. Ask at the counter; **don't budget on it.**

### 1d. Matsuyama city / Dogo → the port — ⚠️ the limousine bus is effectively dead

Iyotetsu official (<https://www.iyotetsu.co.jp/bus/limousine/port/>):
「時刻表以外のリムジンバスは現在、運休しております」 — **there is no city → port
limousine bus in the published timetable.** Ishizaki Kisen adds
「松山市駅から発車するリムジンバスはございませんので、ご注意ください」.

⚠️ **Widely-cited figures are now WRONG.** Perchance to Roam (2022): *"the
Iyotetsu Shuttle Bus from either the Matsuyama JR Train Station (20 mins./750¥)
or the Dogo Onsen tram station (40 mins./940¥)"*; japan-guide still says
*"15 minutes, 920 yen"*. **Do not rely on these.**

**The route that actually works** (Ishizaki Kisen official access page,
<http://www.ishizakikisen.co.jp/contents/matsuyama_access.html>):

- Matsuyamashi Sta → Takahama Sta: Iyotetsu suburban line, **~21 min**
- JR Matsuyama Sta → walk **~10 min** to Otemachi Sta → Takahama, **~19 min**
- **Dogo Onsen** → tram line ⑤ toward JR Matsuyama-eki-mae, **~22 min**, then as above
- Takahama Sta → Matsuyama Kanko Port: **connecting shuttle ~2 min**, or walk 800 m / ~10 min

Fares (adult one-way, from 2026-04-01, cash / IC): shuttle **¥270 / ¥250** ·
Takahama↔Otemachi **¥460 / ¥440** · Takahama↔Matsuyamashi **¥520 / ¥500** ·
city tram flat **¥250 / ¥230**.

Shuttle frequency and its match to each sailing: **not stated**.

**Dogo luggage hack:** 「道後温泉手ぶら観光便」 — baggage delivery between Matsuyama
Kanko Port and 15 named Dogo ryokan, Apr–Sep 2026, **¥700 per piece one-way**
(max 200 cm, 15 kg).
[PDF](https://setonaikaikisen.co.jp/cruise/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2023/06/a5e6242287f1424e52c8891e8622ffaf.pdf)

## 2. Okunoshima (大久野島, Rabbit Island)

📍 [Tadanoumi Port](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E5%BF%A0%E6%B5%B7%E6%B8%AF) ·
[Tadanoumi Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E5%BF%A0%E6%B5%B7%E9%A7%85) ·
[Okunoshima](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E5%A4%A7%E4%B9%85%E9%87%8E%E5%B3%B6)

### 2a. TWO ferry operators — this is why sources disagree on the fare

Confirmed by Hiroshima Prefecture's official tourism site:
<https://dive-hiroshima.com/en/feature/usagi-okunoshima/>

**(i) Omishima Ferry (大三島フェリー / Sanyo Shosen)** — the car ferry.
Timetable <http://sanyo-shosen.jp/omishima/time.html> · fares
<http://sanyo-shosen.jp/omishima/fare.html>
*(**http only** — the site's TLS cert is broken, browsers may refuse https)*

Tadanoumi↔Okunoshima (tariff from 1 June 2021): **adult ¥360 one-way /
¥720 return; child ¥180 / ¥360. Bicycle ¥120.**

From Tadanoumi, **calling at Okunoshima**: 08:30, 09:40, 10:50, 12:00, 14:05,
15:15, 16:25. *(The 07:30, 17:35, 19:10 run direct to Moriguchi and **skip**
Okunoshima.)*
From Okunoshima: 08:20, 09:20, 10:30, 11:40, 13:48, 14:56, 16:06, and
**17:16 — February to October only.**

**(ii) Kyukamura Kyakusen (休暇村客船)** — passenger boat, Tadanoumi–Okunoshima
only. Adult one-way **¥310**, child ¥160. Its timetable PDF is Cloudflare-blocked
(<https://www.qkamura.or.jp/ohkuno/access/images/time.pdf>) — **exact times not
stated**; call Tadanoumi Port **0846-26-0853** (07:00–19:45 daily).

**Combined:** the official port site says services leave **"roughly every 30–45
minutes"**, crossing **15 minutes** (<http://rabbit-island.info/en/>). Hiroshima
Prefecture: **last departure from Tadanoumi for day visitors is 18:30.**

> **Fare confusion resolved:** japan-guide and NAVITIME quote **¥310** (the
> Kyukamura boat); the Omishima car ferry is **¥360**. Anyone quoting ¥620
> return is citing a pre-2021 fare.

**Cash only.** Official EN: *"Japan Rail Pass accepted up to Tadanoumi JR
Station but not for ferry services. Cash (JPY) only."*

**Bonus route:** a **"Rabbit Line" high-speed boat from Mihara Port** — 6/day,
**weekends and holidays only, 30 min, ¥1,500**.

It beats the 2-hour Kure Line local on time and comfort at roughly the same

### 2c. Luggage — solved, and cheaply
Official port site: *"A short-term luggage keeping service is available…
**500 yen per bag / day.** Open during ferry operating hours, 365 days a year."*
The port's 2026-07-20 notice is a fee revision for exactly this service, so
¥500 is current. Confirmed by Ms Travel Solo, who paid ¥500 at the terminal shop.
<https://www.mstravelsolo.com/okunoshima-island-japan/>

Alternative: Nick & Jerry used **coin lockers at Mihara Station** and
day-tripped from there.
<https://www.nickandjerry.com/blog/japan-2023-day-13-okunoshima-island-aka-rabbit-island>

### 2d. Rabbit food — ⚠️ buy it BEFORE you board
GetHiroshima: rabbit food is *"no longer sold on the island (get yours from the
Tadanoumi port)."* Nick & Jerry bought bags for **¥200** at the Rabbit Island
store by Tadanoumi station and confirm *"There wasn't anywhere to buy rabbit
food on the island."* Ms Travel Solo **regretted** not buying any.
Port shop open **7:00–19:45, 365 days**.

**Official rules** ([JP](http://rabbit-island.info/) / [EN](http://rabbit-island.info/en/)):
- **Do not hold or chase rabbits** — they panic and can break bones; no vet on the island.
- **Do not feed on roads** — feeding rabbits don't notice vehicles.
- **Do not leave leftovers** — damp pellets go uneaten, spoiled veg attracts
  crows, and food near a burrow tells crows where the kits are.
- **No human food** — bread, snacks, and *any* potato/sweet-potato make them ill.
- Don't cycle recklessly; don't take rabbits home (illegal); don't abandon pets there.
- **Check under parked cars** before moving them.
- **Please refill the water pans.**

> The port explicitly recommends **pellets**. The "cabbage is cheapest" advice
> in blog aggregators contradicts the official "no leftovers, veg spoils" line.

### 2e. On the island
Only one hotel: **Kyukamura Okunoshima**. Ms Travel Solo's first-hand record:
restaurant **Usanchu 11:30–13:30**; **Usanchu Cafe 10:00–18:00**;
**Poison Gas Museum ¥150**; **onsen day-use ¥550**. She brought a picnic.
Nick & Jerry: *"Beyond one hotel restaurant, there's no other food option there"*
— they bought pastries the night before.

Rental bikes at the hotel **¥400–600 guests / ¥600–800 day visitors**
(dive-hiroshima). **Free shuttle from the pier to the hotel**, timed to
sailings, no reservation — or a **15-minute walk**.

### 2f. Day trip vs overnight — real reports
- ⚠️ **Three hours is not enough.** Nick & Jerry allocated three and it failed:
  Jerry *"spent waaaaay too much time feeding rabbits"* and they *"ended up
  hustling back to the port,"* concluding they *"really needed like 5 hours."*
- Ms Travel Solo did ~3 hours (perimeter, museum, observatory, ruins) and still
  called it *"one of the best day trips"* she'd taken.
- todolist-japan recommends **9–11 hours door to door** from Hiroshima with
  **3–4 hours minimum** on the island.
- **The case for staying:** rabbits are crepuscular —
  「うさぎは夜行性なので、夕方と早朝に活発に行動します」. Day-trippers see the least
  active rabbits.
- **Kyukamura rates: not stated** (site Cloudflare-blocked). Official:
  <https://www.qkamura.or.jp/ohkuno/>. The port page warns *"advance hotel
  reservation is advised as accommodation is limited."* There is also a
  **campsite** — 10 fenced grass pitches by the sea.

## 3. Takehara old town (竹原)

📍 [Takehara Machinami Hozon Chiku](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E7%AB%B9%E5%8E%9F%E7%94%BA%E4%B8%A6%E4%BF%9D%E5%AD%98%E5%9C%B0%E5%8C%BA)

One stop area west of Tadanoumi on the same JR Kure Line — genuinely *en route*,
not a detour.

- **Access** ([official city tourism board](https://www.takeharakankou.jp/en/access/shigai/)):
  Hiroshima Sta → JR Kure Line → Takehara Sta, **approx. 1.5–2 hours**;
  Kaguya-hime express bus from Hiroshima Sta, **approx. 1 h 10 min**.
  Geiyo Bus gives **Takehara Station ¥1,300** adult one-way.
- **Walk from Takehara Station to the old town: 12 minutes**
  ([Japan Experience](https://www.japan-experience.com/all-about-japan/hiroshima/attractions-excursions/takehara))
- **What's there:** the Townscape Preservation District (Important Preservation
  District for Groups of Traditional Buildings) — Kasai House, Mitsumoto House,
  Morikawa House, Saiho-ji temple with a viewing deck, cafes and galleries, plus
  a museum to **Masataka Taketsuru**, Takehara native and founder of Nikka
  Whisky. Nicknamed "Little Kyoto of Aki".
- **How long: not stated** as a firm number anywhere. Japan Experience describes
  *"a leisurely afternoon."*
- Merchant-house opening hours and admission fees: **not stated** on English pages.

> **Verdict:** 12 minutes' walk from a station you're passing anyway, so the
> marginal cost is one train's wait. Nobody reports it as a highlight worth
> building a day around; nobody reports regretting an hour or two there.

## 4. Shimanami Kaido as an exit from Shikoku

📍 [Imabari Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E4%BB%8A%E6%B2%BB%E9%A7%85) ·
[Onomichi Station](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E5%B0%BE%E9%81%93%E9%A7%85) ·
[Setoda Port](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E7%80%AC%E6%88%B8%E7%94%B0%E6%B8%AF)

### 4a. ⚠️ There is NO direct Imabari→Onomichi Shimanami Liner
The Onomichi line was **abolished 20 June 2005** (ridership had fallen to 5.5
passengers per trip). Mihara closed 1999; Innoshima suspended 1999.
[ja.wikipedia しまなみライナー](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%97%E3%81%BE%E3%81%AA%E3%81%BF%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%83%8A%E3%83%BC)
Setouchi Bus offers only Hiroshima and Fukuyama lines plus an "Imabari–Onomichi
**transfer guide**" — and that guide page is **currently 404 on their own site**
(<https://www.setouchibus.co.jp/highway/onomichi.html>). Live site defect.

### 4b. What actually runs

**Shimanami Liner, Imabari → Fukuyama** — <https://www.setouchibus.co.jp/highway/fukuyama.html>
**1 h 39 min, ¥2,800** adult · **~16 departures daily each way** ·
**no reservation needed** (「予約は必要ございません」) · bicycles only if folded and
bagged · calls at Oshima, Hakatajima, Omishima, Setoda PA, Innoshima Ohashi.

**⭐ Shimanami Liner, Imabari → Hiroshima DIRECT** — <https://www.setouchibus.co.jp/highway/hiroshima.html>
**2 h 50 min, ¥4,200** one-way (¥7,600 return, valid 10 days) ·
**3 departures daily each way** (6/day weekends and holidays per Wikipedia) ·
**all seats reserved, advance purchase required**, bookable from 1 month ahead
until 30 min before · **no bicycles at all** · terminates Hiroshima Bus Center
or Hiroshima Station shinkansen exit.

> This is the option most people miss: **Imabari → Hiroshima, ¥4,200, no boat,
> no shinkansen surcharge, crosses the whole Shimanami Kaido by road.**

**Imabari → Onomichi by bus, the actual method** —
[touring-shimanami.com/en/bus-route-e/](https://touring-shimanami.com/en/bus-route-e/)
- *Option 1:* Shimanami Liner (Fukuyama line) to **Innoshima Ohashi BS, ~1.5 h**,
  then Innoshima–Onomichi **local bus (~18 services/day)** to Onomichi Station.
  ⚠️ *"Transferring buses at Innoshima Ohashi BS may not be smooth depending on
  the time of day"* — long waits, no facilities at the stop.
- *Option 2:* Shimanami Liner to **Mukaihigashi BS**, walk **10–15 min** to the
  Mukaishima pier, then the ¥100 ferry across.
- **japan-guide's published total for Option 1:** *"Depending on connection
  times, the entire trip takes **1.5 to 2 hours and costs 2,390 yen**. There are
  hourly connections."* <https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3478.html>

## 5. Route comparison — budget, no car, no surcharges

### Route (a) — Matsuyama ferry → Hiroshima, then backtrack east to Tadanoumi
Dogo → port: tram ¥250 + Iyotetsu ¥520 + shuttle ¥270 = **¥1,040** ·
Cruise Ferry **¥5,800** (2h40) or Linear Jet **¥8,800** (70 min) ·
Hiroden **¥240** · then Hiroshima → Tadanoumi: **Kaguya-hime bus ¥1,500 /
1h40**, or Kure Line local **¥1,320 / 2 h**.

**Cheapest published total Dogo → Tadanoumi: ¥8,400–8,580.**

*Pros:* one long scenic relaxing boat; drops you in the city you'll sleep in;
the Kaguya-hime bus makes the backtrack painless and lets you base in Hiroshima
and day-trip out. *Cons:* the ferry is the single most expensive item in the
whole chain; you cover the Hiroshima–Tadanoumi corridor twice.

### Route (b) — Imabari / Shimanami → Onomichi → Mihara → Tadanoumi → Hiroshima
No cycling: Imabari → Innoshima Ohashi → Onomichi **¥2,390, 1.5–2 h** —
*plus* Matsuyama → Imabari (**not stated** here).
Or Imabari → Fukuyama **¥2,800 / 1h39**, then JR local back west.
Cycling: bike **¥3,000 + ¥1,000 one-way = ¥4,000**, bridges free to Mar 2028,
plus the ¥110 Mukaishima ferry.
Semi-cycling: Onomichi ↔ Setoda **¥1,500 + ¥500 bike**, 6 sailings/day — the
classic "ride half, sail half" compromise.
Then Mihara → Tadanoumi **¥320 / 22 min**, Tadanoumi → Hiroshima **¥1,320–1,500**.

*Pros:* geographically linear, **no backtracking**; **Imabari→Onomichi by bus at
¥2,390 is less than half the cheapest Matsuyama ferry**; deposits you at
Onomichi and Mihara, both natural Okunoshima bases. *Cons:* the Innoshima Ohashi
transfer is the weak link (operator's own partner site warns connections "may
not be smooth", nothing at the stop); more moving parts.

### ⭐ Route (b-bis) — Imabari → Hiroshima direct bus, ¥4,200
**2 h 50 min, 3/day (6/day weekends), Hiroshima Bus Center.** Cheaper than the
Cruise Ferry, sees the bridges, needs no train at all. **Requires reservation.**
Then Kaguya-hime bus out to Tadanoumi.

### Route (c) — via Takamatsu → Okayama → Mihara
Every leg is shinkansen or Marine Liner + Sanyo Line; the Okayama→Mihara local
segment has **no published time or fare** I could source. This exists mainly for
JR-pass holders. **For a surcharge-avoiding budget traveller it has no
advantage** — longest way round, and its cheap version is a long chain of locals.

### What travellers actually say
- r/JapanTravel 2023-04-29 snippet: *"I found it easiest to take the ferry from
  mihara (not tadanoumi). Longer but lined up with the shinkansen better."*
  → supports basing at **Mihara** if coming from the east.
- Reinhard (Perchance to Roam) argues the slow ferry over the fast one on value
  grounds — relevant if you pick route (a).

> **Read of the evidence:** **(b)** or **(b-bis)** is cheaper and avoids doubling
> back. **(a)** is the more pleasant single day and puts you straight into your
> Hiroshima base — and the **¥1,500 Kaguya-hime bus** largely neutralises the
> backtrack objection. **(c) has nothing to recommend it here.**

### 6b. The visitor tax
[Hatsukaichi City official](https://www.city.hatsukaichi.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/110/59551.html)
- **¥100 per person per visit**, or **¥500 per person per year** (annual option
  must be arranged in advance at Hatsukaichi City Hall / Miyajima or Ono branches)
- Started **1 October 2023**; **collected by the ferry operator, added to the
  ticket.** IC gate touch deducts fare + tax together.
- Exempt: residents, commuters (48+ hrs/month), local students, preschool
  children, school groups and leaders, disability certificate holders.
- **Rail Pass holders still pay the ¥100** — the pass covers the JR fare only.

**⭐ The best budget instrument by far — Hiroden 一日乗車乗船券**
([official](https://www.hiroden.co.jp/ticket-pass/bargain-tickets/oneday.html)):
- **¥1,000 adult / ¥550 child**
- Covers **all Hiroden streetcar lines + the Miyajima Matsudai ferry**
- **The ¥100 visitor tax is included**
- **Discounts the Misen ropeway round trip from ¥2,000 to ¥1,500**

One pass covers a Miyajima day *and* all city trams *and* the tax *and* saves
¥500 on the ropeway. (Streetcar-only day pass: ¥700 — no ferry, no ropeway discount.)

**High-speed boat from the Peace Park** — Aqua Net "World Heritage Sea Route",
<https://www.aqua-net-h.co.jp/en/heritage/> — **45 min, ¥2,400 one-way /
¥4,400 return**, 17 departures each way daily, visitor tax **not** included.
Scenic and time-saving, but ~10× the tram+ferry cost. **Not a budget choice.**

### 6d. Ropeway and Mt Misen — the honest version for people who dislike hiking

<https://miyajima-ropeway.info/fare/>
**Adult ¥2,000 round trip / ¥1,100 one way** (child ¥1,000 / ¥550;
seniors 65+ and groups 15+ ¥1,800). Two stages: **Momijidani → Kayatani
(~10 min) → Shishiiwa (~4 min)**, **mandatory transfer**; ~20 min total.
**Ascent 9:00–16:00** — the 16:00 last ascent catches people out.
**Free shuttle bus to Momijidani Station from 9:50, roughly every 20 minutes.**

⚠️ **The walk to the summit is real, and the official page says so.**
Miyajima Ropeway's own fare page: 「弥山（みせん）山頂へはさらに往復約1時間かかります」 —
**about one hour round trip on top of the ropeway.**

japan-guide is blunter: *"From the ropeway's upper station at Shishi-iwa
Observatory, it is still **about a 30 minute walk up to the summit along a
rather steep hiking trail**"* — roughly **1 km with 100 m of elevation gain**.
<https://www.japan-guide.com/e/e3451.html>
Independently confirmed at 30 minutes by [Japan Uncharted](https://japanuncharted.com/hiroshima/mountain/misen-ropeway-and-walk),
[Snow Monkey Resorts](https://www.snowmonkeyresorts.com/activities/exploring-mount-misen/),
[Japan Travel](https://en.japantravel.com/hiroshima/mount-misen-miyajima/3128) and
[JOC Japan Travel](https://www.jocjapantravel.com/chugoku-miyajima-mount-misen/).
Grow Adventurous, who did it **with children aged 3 and 4**, describe it as
*"not particularly long, but it does include a fair amount of elevation gain and
many steps."*

> **Recommendation for these travellers:** ride the ropeway, enjoy **Shishiiwa
> observatory** (itself an observatory with Inland Sea views), and treat the
> summit as strictly optional. Whether Shishiiwa alone "is enough" is
> **not stated** definitively by any source — but the summit adds ~1 hour and
> ~100 m of climbing on a steep, stepped trail.

**Walking up instead** (not for these two): all three trails — Momijidani,
Daisho-in, Omoto — take **1.5 to 2 hours** to the summit. Daisho-in *"offers the
nicest views and is not as steep as the other two."*

**Current status (Aug 2026):** Reikado hall burned **20 May 2026** (fire out
after 27.5 hours). **All Misen entry restrictions lifted and the ropeway resumed
normal operation 21 May 2026** —
[Miyajima Tourist Association](https://www.miyajima.or.jp/new/mibunrui/%E3%80%90%E3%81%8A%E7%9F%A5%E3%82%89%E3%81%9B%E3%80%91%E5%BC%A5%E5%B1%B1%E5%85%A5%E5%B1%B1%E8%A6%8F%E5%88%B6%E3%83%BB%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%97%E3%82%A6%E3%82%A8%E3%83%BC%E9%81%8B%E4%BC%91/),
[Chugoku Shimbun](https://www.chugoku-np.co.jp/articles/-/834642).

### 6e. Where budget travellers sleep
**The case for the island** — Delightful Travel Notes,
<https://delightfultravelnotes.com/miyajima-overnight-stay/>: once day visitors
left by 5 pm *"the atmosphere changed completely"*; streets quiet, shops shut,
early mornings *"especially peaceful"* with the torii reflected in still water.
They valued seeing the gate at **different tidal states** across a full cycle.
Stayed at Kinsuikan and call it *"a splurge."*

Their warnings, which matter here:
- **Evening dining on the island is scarce — book dinner with your accommodation.**
- **Forward large suitcases ahead; local trains lack storage.**
- **Daisho-in Temple involves "quite a few stairs."**

**The case against** — TripAdvisor Hiroshima forum: *"if it's just a bed for the
night then return to Hiroshima as you will have more choices available"*
(quoted from snippet; thread 403s to automated fetching).

**Named budget options on the island:** **Miyajima Guest House Mikuniya**
(japan-guide categorises it "Budget-friendly", traditional Japanese-style);
**Sakuraya Ryokan** (*"the most accessible traditional experience on the island
for budget-conscious travelers"*). **Prices: not stated.**

**Miyajimaguchi** is barely covered in first-person accounts — a ferry pier and
a station, little else.

> **Budget verdict:** with the **¥1,000 Hiroden all-in-one day pass** covering
> trams + ferry + tax, and JR ferries running to 22:42, a day trip from
> Hiroshima is cheap and elastic. Staying on the island buys the empty-island
> dawn and dusk — genuinely praised by everyone who did it — at a real premium
> and with a dinner-booking constraint.
